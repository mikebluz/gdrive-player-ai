← mikeluz Don’t Fret chords on the fretboard, and every chord a stack of notes could be
Numbers are fingers — 1 index, 2 middle, 3 ring, 4 little. A bar across several strings is one finger laid flat. × means don’t play that string, ○ means play it open.

Name a stack of notes

One note per box, from low to high — the first is the bass, and each note stacks upward from the one before it. Sharps C# and flats Bb both work.
