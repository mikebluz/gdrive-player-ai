Thirteen directions for the new homepage← the current homepage

Same artist. Thirteen different pages.

Each is a working page, not a mockup — click into them, they do what they say. They are deliberately far apart, so pick the one you want to move toward and we sharpen from there (or take one thing from each).

OS — from your reference

The page is a desktop

Draggable windows, a taskbar with a real clock, desktop icons that reopen what you closed. Content lives in windows instead of a scroll, and the page doesn't scroll at all — you rearrange it. Same family as the site you sent, built from your material.

Drag the title bars · close and reopen from the icons
A

The page is an instrument

Generates music on arrival; the type is driven by what's sounding. Space re-rolls it. The one direction no other musician can copy — it's your software.

Needs sound on · try space and m
B

Terminal

You type to get around: listen, watch, shop, code, who. Scanlines, a real prompt, no buttons anywhere.

Every command works · try help
C

Hand-made

Your drawn wordmark and the Air Bath artwork, taped to a sheet of paper. Cut-out letters, nothing quite square, nothing centred.

Uses art you already have
D

Brutalist

No image, no gradient, no rounded corners. GRAINS at 17rem, hairline rules, links as raw underlined text. The video takes the whole screen when asked.

Loads instantly · hover the letters
E

VHS

Analog decay instead of digital: tape wobble, a rolling tracking band, chroma bleed on the title, a timecode that actually counts. Warm and degraded — a dub of a dub.

Where B is cold, this is warm
F

Gig poster

A screen-printed show bill. Two inks that overprint where they cross, a halftone dot screen, condensed type stacked in a heavy rule box. Wants to be stapled to a pole.

Loud where D is stark
G

Editorial

The only light one. A magazine spread — serif text, a drop cap, real columns, an image bleeding off the edge. Quiet and confident; the opposite bet from shouting.

Cream paper · wants real writing
H

The page is a player

The homepage is one piece of software: title bar, LCD, spectrum, playlist. Not a site about a musician who codes — a small application made by one. The video opens in its screen.

Everything in the list is real
I

Archive

Not a hero page — an index. Records and software in one filterable table, because they're one body of work. The bet: the range is the impressive part, and a single-single page hides it.

New work is just a new row
J

The nav is an instrument

Where A played music at you, this hands you the instrument. Every tile is a note AND a destination — pentatonic, so you can't play a wrong one. Keys A–L work; drag to glissando.

Needs sound on · genuinely playable
K

Scroll

The first that isn't one screen. "I couldn't find the sound, so I built the instrument, then I made a record with it." Pacing does the work — it has a beginning and a punchline.

The software finally earns its place
L

Cartridge

From your own Air Bath NES art: a console title screen. Arrow keys move the cursor, enter selects, the video plays inside the bezel. Software as something you PLAY, not something you use.
