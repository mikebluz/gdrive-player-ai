Bloops — Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

Bloops is a music-making app. It is built to work without collecting anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Bloops has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no servers of ours that receive your data. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.

Where your music lives

Projects, sequences, and recordings you make are stored on your device. Nothing you create leaves your device unless you export or share it yourself.

Google Drive (optional)

Bloops can optionally connect to your Google Drive to stream your own audio files and back up your projects. If you choose to sign in, authentication is handled by Google; your files move directly between your device and Google Drive. We never see them, and no access token or file passes through any server of ours. You can use the app fully without ever connecting Drive.

Microphone (optional)

Bloops can use the microphone to turn humming into melodies and to record audio, only when you start a recording. Audio recorded stays on your device like everything else. The app never records without an explicit action from you.

Children

Bloops collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
